# Creative Team — Task Pull System Guidelines

## Where Tasks Come From
All incoming design tasks are assigned to the **DR Team: Creative** Basecamp project. This is your queue. Do not wait to be assigned — it is your responsibility to pull from it.

---

## How to Claim a Task
1. Go to the Creative project and review the unassigned task list
2. Pick a task you can realistically start and complete given your current workload
3. **Assign it to yourself** in Basecamp
4. Add the **HDD** (hard due date) to the task title using this format:
   `[Task Name] | HDD: 6/13`
5. Set your **time estimate** in Everhour for that task
6. Richard will review your picks each morning

---

## Your Daily Capacity
You have **6.5 hours of task-focused time per day.** The remaining 1.5 hours covers follow-ups, Slack, internal check-ins, and admin.

When pulling tasks, estimate against 6.5h — not your full 8h day.

**Daily Everhour logging:**
- Log all assigned task time against the specific Basecamp task
- Log your daily admin/misc time as a single entry: **"Misc / Admin"** — no other labels
- Everything should total 8h by end of day

---

## How Many to Pull
- Pull **1–3 tasks at a time** — only what you can realistically work on this week
- Do not claim tasks just to hold them. If it is not starting within 2 business days, do not pull it yet
- Check back daily — new tasks come in throughout the week

---

## Pulling a Balanced Set
The queue is sorted **oldest first** — that is your starting point, not your preference.

- **Start with the oldest tasks** in the queue, not the ones that look easiest or most familiar
- Your set of 1–3 tasks should not all be the same type — mix a heavier lift with a lighter one if available
- **Do not skip a task** because it looks complex, unfamiliar, or undesirable — flag it with a comment if you genuinely think you are the wrong fit, but do not just leave it and grab the next one
- If you notice you keep pulling the same task types, that is a signal to branch out

**What Richard is watching for:**
- Tasks aging in the queue while newer ones get pulled
- One designer consistently taking only short or easy tasks
- The same task types always going to the same person

The goal is even distribution across deadline pressure, task complexity, and client type — not just filling your plate with comfortable work.

---

## Capacity Check Before You Pull
Before claiming a task, ask yourself:
- What do I already have assigned with an HDD this week?
- Do I have enough hours between now and the HDD to complete this task? (Remember: 6.5h/day, not 8h)
- If you are unsure, pull fewer tasks, not more

---

## When You Are At Capacity
- If your plate is full, **do not pull new tasks**
- Leave them in the queue for a teammate
- If everything in the queue is urgent and no one has capacity, flag it to Richard — do not silently skip it

---

## What Richard Reviews
Every morning Richard reviews what was self-assigned. He may:
- **Approve** your picks (no action needed from you)
- **Reassign** a task to a different designer based on workload
- **Add context** to a task via comment if the brief needs clarification

You do not need to wait for explicit approval before starting — if it has been assigned to you since yesterday with no change, you are clear to proceed.

---

## Rules Summary
| Rule | What to Do |
|---|---|
| Pull daily | Check the Creative queue every morning |
| Start with the oldest tasks | Age first, not ease first |
| Claim only what fits | Plan against 6.5h/day, not 8h |
| Pull a mixed set | Heavy + light, not all easy |
| Add HDD to title | Always, every task you claim |
| Set estimate in Everhour | Do this when you claim the task |
| Log "Misc / Admin" daily | One entry, 1.5h, every day |
| Do not skip uncomfortable tasks | Flag them, do not silently pass |
| Do not hold tasks you cannot start soon | Leave them for teammates |
| Flag if the queue is urgent and no one has room | Do not let it go unnoticed |
